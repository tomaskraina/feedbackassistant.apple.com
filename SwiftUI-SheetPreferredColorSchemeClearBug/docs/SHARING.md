## Short post

SwiftUI `.preferredColorScheme(nil)` can leave an open sheet stuck in Dark Mode.
Dismiss and reopen it, and it follows the light system appearance again.

Reproduced in Simulator on iOS 18.6, 26.5, 27.0, and 27.2.
Sample project, exact steps, and a short demo:
https://github.com/tomaskraina/feedbackassistant.apple.com/tree/codex/swiftui-sheet-preferred-color-scheme-bug/SwiftUI-SheetPreferredColorSchemeClearBug
